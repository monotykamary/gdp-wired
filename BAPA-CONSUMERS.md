# BAPA quantified-consumer census

Scope: explicit `finite` annotations on one-key guarded universal conditions, and on a whole prenexed law. `finite` is not a trusted proof premise. All guarded bodies continue to mean the old unbounded formula in Lean. This inventory separates **actual evaluation** from syntax/proof processing. Search census command (compact spellings included): `rg -l 'op\s*(===|!==)\s*["\x27]all["\x27]|case\s*["\x27]all["\x27]' core kernel research interchange lean-kernel --glob '*.ts' --glob '!*.test.ts'`. The initial space-dependent pattern missed compact consumers; Fable's review caught it, and the census below was expanded after inspecting them. Also inspected evaluators using `Law.forall`, which do not match that command.

## Proof-side / structure-only consumers (no new rule)

- `kernel/kernel.ts`: scope uses exact guard validation plus immutable-parameter dependency check before bypassing `keyOnly`; legacy `closing`, `assumed`, instantiation, substitution, frame building, map-key equality and count checking are unchanged. Finite annotation on bulk writes is rejected.
- `kernel/prove.ts`, `kernel/reader.ts`, `kernel/epr.ts`, `core/cyclic.ts`, `core/kind.ts`: proof-side instantiation, goal reading, symbolic quantifier treatment and translations. The guarded condition remains in every hypothesis. These do not decide finite truth by enumerating runtime breakpoints. Proposals are checked by the existing kernel.
- `core/dsl.ts`: preserves markers when hoisting; whole-law prenexing retains the marker and original guard; refuses nested/combined positive finite laws and finite step laws. `sizedSort(...).every/some` is the only new front-end entry; legacy `everyKey`, `everyBelow`, `someKey` unchanged.
- `core/compose.ts`, `core/relational.ts`, `core/exists.ts` substitution, `lean-kernel/qalpha.ts`: capture-avoiding transformations retain the annotation and transform the guard/body together. Later scope checks revalidate the result. No domain is stored twice.
- `lean-kernel/emit.ts`, `lean-kernel/lean-terms.ts`: export the guarded body to the old natural universal. The runtime annotation has no trusted meaning in the twin. `Sets.sOk` still enforces distinctness and flag/witness avoidance; it has no breakpoint-shape premise.
- `core/carry.ts`, `core/count-case-compose.ts`, `core/count-copy-shape.ts`, `core/count-demand.ts`, `core/count-facts.ts`, `core/count-guard-shape.ts`, `core/count-guard-tree.ts`, `core/count-leader.ts`, `core/count-premise-view.ts`, `core/count-recovery/scalar-guards.ts`, `core/count-replacement.ts`, `core/count-row-agreement.ts`, `core/count-row-copy-linear.ts`, `core/count-row-copy.ts`, `core/count-row-policy.ts`, `core/counts.ts`, `core/count-support.ts`, `core/count-unions.ts`, `core/count-witness-flags.ts`, `core/count-write-shape.ts`, `core/quorum-laws.ts`: symbolic count/candidate extraction, refusal/ignoring of uninstantiated local binders, or delegation to common evaluators. Not authorities for truth; all proposals remain checked. No new count rule in this slice.
- `core/count-focused.ts`: proposes ordinary guarded universal instances; `core/count-cases.ts`: removes whole instance tuples before rechecking unspecialized goals; `core/count-frozen-guard.ts`: gives quantified flags their existing symbolic Boolean aliases, not sampled truth.
- `core/count-lifted-increment.ts`, `core/count-lifted-replacement.ts`: supply ordinary instances to `countedObligations`; candidate/branch search still builds and checks certificates.
- `core/count-recovery/action-guard.ts`: untrusted action/read proposals using symbolic flag aliases; `field-bound-lemmas.ts`: source-bound candidates, not assumed axioms; `live-dependencies.ts`: selects source hypotheses and explicitly excludes quantifiers from scalar context; `member-inclusions.ts`: matches guarded source shapes and proposes point lemmas; `row-cases.ts`: read/write branch strategy selection with symbolic aliases. These are not runtime finite truth authorities, and their proposed facts still require pointwise/count checking.
- `core/strategy-premises.ts`: alpha-normalized strategy identity retains the entire guarded body. It ignores operational marker spelling, intentionally: equal guarded formulas have equal logical denotation. It does not evaluate them or grant a new proof rule.
- `core/finite-frontiers.ts`, `core/live.ts`, `core/cycles.ts`, `core/transfer.ts`: syntax-derived proposals/formatting or delegation to common evaluation; not complete covers or proofs.
- `research/dag/{goalio,profile,tree,vars}.ts`, `research/data/show.ts`, `research/egraph/{indexed-match,ite,match,trigger-index}.ts`, `research/perf/prof/show.ts`, `research/protocols/enum/probe.ts`, `research/track-wall/frame-cut.ts`: quantifier refusal, formatting, symbolic canonicalisation or already-closed proof processing; no finite decision procedure.
- `research/hw/{synth,synth-more}.ts`, `research/synth/{compile,compile-more,synth}.ts`, `research/table/frontier.ts`: restricted synthesizers/emitters; their condition compilation refuses quantifiers, not approximate marked evaluation. They do not receive a prenexed finite law as their generated linear candidate.
- `research/lane-i/rolling-20261111/{alpha-source,c2-alpha-audit}.ts`, `research/track-gen/t14-stutter.ts`, `research/verify-all/{capture-alpha,paxos-feature-census,paxos-law12-alpha}.ts`, `research/track-p/{seven-seventeen/audit,ten-sixtysix/audit,ten-sixtysix/source-probe,profile-step,audit-support,family,thirtythree/audit}.ts`, `research/mldr-incl/pointwise.ts`, `research/egraph/{recover-flags,context-bindings}.ts`, and `research/track-m/{row-cases,thin-source,field-bound-lemmas,section15-control,live-dependencies,focused-loop,action-guard,focused-replay}.ts`: experimental symbolic source analysis/proposals, not production runtime or certificate authority; no edits or finite-completeness claim for these historical experiments.

## Regression contract

A false value only at the non-breakpoint key 17 must be found by interpreter, closure compiler, generated JS, law sampling with bound 4, and watches. Guard mutations (no guard, wrong binder, <= instead of <, self-dependent size) and mutable sizes are refused. Empty domains and sizes 65/129 are exercised. Interchange and specialized screeners refuse rather than silently downgrade. Tests must also prove that legacy unmarked certificates and quantifiers stay accepted, and actual marked-machine Lean exports must compile using the unchanged checker.
